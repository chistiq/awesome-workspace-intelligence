# Governance

Awesome Workspace Intelligence is a community-curated reference, not a
directory, ranking, or endorsement program.

## Decision principles

Maintainers evaluate contributions using the published scope and review
criteria:

1. relevance to understanding or governing a software workspace;
2. practical or lasting educational value;
3. a primary, accessible source;
4. a factual description that explains why the resource is useful;
5. sufficient documentation to evaluate the resource;
6. evidence of maintenance, or explicit historical significance.

Stars, company size, sponsorship, and maintainer affiliation are not automatic
acceptance or rejection criteria.

## Conflicts of interest

Self-submissions are welcome. Contributors and reviewers must disclose a
material affiliation with the submitted resource. A maintainer should avoid
being the sole approver of a resource they own or represent when another
reviewer is available.

Workspai is evaluated under the same criteria as every other project.

## Changes and removals

Entries may be corrected, moved, labeled historical, or removed when they
become unsafe, misleading, inaccessible, abandoned without lasting reference
value, or outside the list's scope. Removal is not a judgment on the people
behind a project.

Substantial taxonomy changes should be proposed separately from resource
additions so they can be reviewed on their own merits.

## Appeals

If a contribution is declined, the contributor may open a focused issue with
new evidence or a clearer scope argument. Maintainers should explain decisions
against published criteria and remain open to correction.
